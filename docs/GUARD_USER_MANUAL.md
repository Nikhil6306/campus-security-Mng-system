# Campus Security Guard User Manual
## Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for Gate & Perimeter Security

**University:** Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya (DSVV), Haridwar  
**User Role:** Gate Security Guards, Duty Officers, Shift Supervisors  

---

## 1. Introduction

This manual defines the standard operational procedures for security officers stationed at university access gates (Main Gate, Hostel Gate, VIP Gate, Staff Entrance). All gate officers must operate through the **Campus Security Guard Mobile App** or the Gate Desk Terminal.

---

## 2. Daily Gate Workflow & Procedures

### 2.1 Starting Your Shift
1. Arrive at the guard booth 15 minutes prior to shift commencement.
2. Open the **Campus Security Guard** application on your assigned device.
3. Verify your credentials and log in.
4. On the Dashboard, verify that the Gate designation matches your post.
5. Tap **Clock In Shift** to record your active duty status.

---

### 2.2 Standard Visitor Entry Procedure (Pre-Booked Visitor)
When a visitor approaches the entrance gate:

1. **Greet & Inquire:** Politely ask the visitor: *"Welcome to DSVV. Do you have an approved visitor pass or meeting reference?"*
2. **Scan the Pass:**
   * Open the app and tap **Scan Visitor QR**.
   * Ask the visitor to hold up their digital pass on their phone screen.
   * Align the camera over the QR code until the device beeps or vibrates.
3. **Verify Identity:**
   * The app will display the visitor's live record and portrait photograph.
   * **Visual Check:** Confirm that the person at the gate strictly matches the photograph on your screen.
   * **Host Check:** Confirm the faculty host or department they are visiting.
   * **Status Check:** Confirm the status pill is green (**APPROVED**).
4. **Vehicle Check (if applicable):**
   * If the visitor is driving, inspect the vehicle registration plate against the number displayed on the app.
5. **Execute Check-In:**
   * Tap **CHECK IN**.
   * Confirm the gate name in the confirmation pop-up.
   * Hand the visitor their temporary vehicle pass or campus badge.
   * Open the barrier arm and direct the visitor to the designated parking area.

---

### 2.3 Walk-in Visitor Registration Procedure (No Prior Booking)
If a visitor arrives without pre-booking:

1. On the Dashboard, tap **New Check-in** (`/check-in`).
2. Collect the visitor's Full Name, Mobile Number, and purpose of visit.
3. Select the department or host they wish to visit.
4. Enter vehicle details if entering with a vehicle.
5. Tap **Capture Photo** to take a live photo using the device camera.
6. Submit the record. The app will generate a walk-in entry pass and log their check-in immediately.

---

### 2.4 Visitor Exit Procedure (Check-Out)
When a visitor exits the campus:

1. Ask the visitor to present their pass QR code, or search their name/ID via **Check-out** (`/check-out`).
2. Scan the pass or select the visitor from the list of currently inside guests.
3. Confirm that the status is **INSIDE**.
4. Collect any issued visitor badges, parking tags, or RFID cards.
5. Tap **CHECK OUT**.
6. The app updates their status to **COMPLETED**, closes the visit record, and reduces the on-campus visitor count.

---

## 3. Emergency SOS Protocol

In the event of an urgent security incident at the gate:
1. Tap the **Red Siren (SOS)** button at the top right of the dashboard.
2. Select the incident category:
   * **Medical Emergency:** Immediate ambulance dispatch.
   * **Fire Hazard:** Fire response team alerting.
   * **Perimeter Intrusion / Unauthorized Entry:** Security supervisor dispatch.
   * **Disturbance / Unrest:** Quick Response Team (QRT) notification.
3. Tap **Broadcast Emergency Alert**.
4. An audible alert and instant high-priority notification will immediately trigger across the Admin Dashboard and all duty officer devices.

---

## 4. Shift Handover Checklist

At the conclusion of each shift:
1. Navigate to **Profile** ➔ **Shift Handover**.
2. Complete the physical inspection checklist:
   - [ ] Walkie-talkie and charger operational.
   - [ ] Metal detector wand functional.
   - [ ] Emergency flashlight charged.
   - [ ] Gate boom barrier remotes accounted for.
   - [ ] Visitor badge inventory reconciled.
3. Enter handover notes regarding any pending visitors, vehicles remaining overnight, or maintenance issues.
4. Both outgoing and incoming guards review and sign off.
5. Tap **Clock Out Shift** and log out.
